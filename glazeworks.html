Glazeworks Privacy Policy

Developer: Genist Games · Contact: genistgames@gmail.com
Applies to the Android app com.genistgames.glazeworks · Last updated: October 28, 2026

Summary

Glazeworks is a single-player puzzle game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no in-app purchases. Your game progress stays on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), which collects certain device data as described below.

Data stored by the game on your device

  • The game stores game progress, the current run, scores and records, unlocked items, the daily-puzzle streak, tutorial state, and sound, music, vibration and language settings in the app's local storage on your device.
  • This data is not sent to Genist Games or to any server we operate. We do not run a server for this game and do not use analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.
  • You can delete this data at any time with Settings → Reset all progress, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app.
  • If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may include this local data in your personal device backup, which is controlled by Google and your device settings.
  • Clipboard: if you tap "Copy result" after a Daily Commission, a short text with your score is copied to your device clipboard. Nothing is sent anywhere by the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows two kinds of ads:

  • Rewarded ads, only when you choose to watch one — for example "Second Wind" (extra tiles after a failed order) or a free workshop reroll. The reward is given only when the ad network confirms that the ad was watched.
  • Interstitial ads, occasionally, at natural breaks, such as the end of a run or the end of a commission. They are frequency-limited and are not shown during your first games.

To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares the following data with Google, according to Google's published disclosure:

DataExamplesPurposes (per Google)
IP addressCan be used to estimate the general location of the deviceAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention

Google states that this data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google's processing is governed by its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure

Consent and your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: where required, the game shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices (ads).
  • Android settings: you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (for example Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on the device).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. The game is fully playable offline; ads and ad rewards are simply unavailable without a connection.

Android permissions

  • Internet and network state — to load ads and detect when the device is offline.
  • Vibration — optional haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings).
  • Advertising ID and Android ad-services permissions — added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising, as described above.
  • Wake lock / foreground service — declared by Google libraries used by the ads SDK for background work scheduling; the game itself does not run a foreground service.

The game does not request access to precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

Glazeworks is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

Sale and sharing of personal data

Genist Games does not collect, sell or rent your personal data. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is processed by Google as described above.

Data retention and deletion

Game data on your device remains until you reset it, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Genist Games holds no copy. For data processed by Google for advertising, use Google's controls linked above or your device's ad settings.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles data (for example, by adding a new service), we will update this page and the "Last updated" date before the change is released.
